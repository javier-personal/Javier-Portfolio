# Known Issues and Risks

1. **Settlement agreement PDF is a public-access risk** if the site is deployed. Do not deploy without explicit approval. (Open, owner decision.)
2. **OCBC internal screenshots and resume** not yet reviewed by owner. (Open.)
3. **OCBC page has no Back button** (owner removed it). Users return via the nav bar. (Intentional, flagged.)
4. **Scroll-to-top never shows on the Internship page**: the page scrolls less than the 200px trigger. (Minor.)
5. **Generated pages overwrite hand edits**: `HTML/ocbc-intern.html`, `internship.html`, `projects.html` and the 13 project pages are regenerated by `tools/build.js`. Make changes in `data/`, `tools/build.js` or `tools/templates/`, not in the output.
6. **Fonts:** OCBC page loads Poppins from Google Fonts (needs network).
7. **Environment limits in Claude's sandbox:** Chromium only, no network; `scroll-behavior: smooth` slows scripted scroll tests.
8. **Task 1-3 pages are hand-written, not generated**, and use CRLF line endings. Keep them out of Prettier.
9. **Resume PDF preview does not load over `file://`** (browser CORS); it works when served over http(s). Pre-existing.
10. **Animations not tested** in Safari/Firefox or on real touch devices; they rely on `IntersectionObserver` and the CSS `translate`/`scale` properties (all current browsers). Without them content simply shows.
11. **Gold text** needs `background-clip: text`; older browsers show plain dark gold text.
12. **Owner hand edits to generated pages** are only safe once copied into `data/projects.json` (done for all differences found on 2026-11-07).
