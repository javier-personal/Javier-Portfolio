Personal Finance Tracker

Created a personal finance app that combines expense tracking, budgeting, savings management and data analytics to help users understand their spending habits, monitor financial progress and make better financial decisions.

My contribution

Independently conceptualised, designed and developed the application, covering its UI/UX, database architecture, authentication, financial management features and interactive data analytics dashboard.

My Development Journey

  1. 01
    The beginning

    Notes app

    Started tracking my finances here, but manual calculations and record-keeping became tedious.

  2. 02
    Automation & analytics

    Excel, Tableau & Power BI

    Automated calculations, organised transactions and tracked debts, then connected the data to Tableau and Power BI to understand my spending habits.

  3. 03
    Design, development & testing

    Full application

    Outgrew spreadsheets and built an app through planning, prototyping, development and multiple rounds of user testing. I gathered feedback, monitored difficulties and refined it across versions before deployment.

From personal need to shared solution What began as a personal solution grew into an opportunity to combine my knowledge of technology and data analytics to help myself, and others to manage their finances.
